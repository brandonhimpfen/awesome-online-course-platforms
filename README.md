## Open Source LMS & Course Engines

Self-hosted, extensible learning management systems and open-source engines for complete platform autonomy and data control.

- [Open edX](https://github.com/openedx/openedx-platform) — Massively scalable open-source course engine powering large-scale MOOCs, corporate academies, and university portals.
- [Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle) — Feature-rich open-source learning management system widely adopted across global educational institutions.
- [Canvas LMS Core](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms) — Open-source core of the Canvas LMS platform, offering modern web interfaces and flexible grading pipelines.
- [ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning/ILIAS) — Modular open-source web-based learning management system supporting integrated test engines and study modules.

## Hosted & Creator Platforms

Cloud-hosted platforms designed for independent creators, digital entrepreneurs, and specialized training businesses.

- [Teachable](https://teachable.com/) — All-in-one platform for building, marketing, and selling online video courses and coaching programs.
- [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/) — Hosted course delivery platform offering customizable storefronts, student communities, and interactive quizzes.
- [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/) — Comprehensive digital business platform integrating course hosting, membership sites, email marketing, and sales funnels.
- [Podia](https://www.podia.com/) — Creator-focused platform for selling online courses, digital downloads, webinars, and community access.

## Enterprise & Higher Ed Systems

Robust cloud LMS platforms engineered for large-scale corporate compliance, workforce training, and university administration.

- [Blackboard Learn](https://www.anthology.com/products/teaching-and-learning/blackboard-learn) — Enterprise learning management solution offering advanced analytics, mobile delivery, and institutional workflows.
- [Brightspace (D2L)](https://www.d2l.com/brightspace/) — Cloud LMS platform focused on personalized learning paths, automated feedback, and accessibility compliance.
- [Absorb LMS](https://www.absorblms.com/) — Enterprise learning management system designed for employee onboarding, compliance tracking, and partner training.

## Platform Extensions & Standards

Tools, protocols, and specialized software integrations that power course delivery networks.

### LTI & Interoperability

- [PyLTI](https://github.com/mitodl/pylti) — Python library implementing 1LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability) specifications for connecting third-party apps into LMS platforms.
- [1EdTech LTI Reference Implementation](https://github.com/1EdTech) — Open standards implementation for secure tool integration and gradebook synchronization.

### Video Hosting & Streaming

- [Video.js](https://github.com/videojs/video.js) — Open-source HTML5 video player framework providing stable playback for HLS and DASH course video streams.
